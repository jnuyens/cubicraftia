Cubicraftia is an open-source 3D sandbox game where you build and survive in a procedurally generated world made of **bricks**. Place bricks on **studs**, customise your **builder**, and explore an infinite terrain — alone or with up to 3 friends over the internet. Sessions are friends-only. The game runs on Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android with full cross-play.

Choose **sandbox** mode for creative building with no danger, or **survival** mode to gather bricks by mining terrain, craft tools, and survive against hostile creatures. Both modes are available in the same session.
