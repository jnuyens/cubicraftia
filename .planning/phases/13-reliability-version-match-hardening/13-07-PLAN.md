<!-- Exact existing patterns to mirror. Do not invent a different wiring mechanism. -->
`src/world/main_scene.gd:_install_parental_gate_wiring()` (around line 1050) is the direct
precedent for dynamically loading and instantiating a UI scene from `main_scene.gd`'s `_ready()`:
`load(path) as PackedScene`, then `.instantiate()`, then `add_child(panel)`, then optionally
mount into `@onready var _ui: CanvasLayer = $UI` for panels that render inside the HUD layer.
This plan adds a sibling method, `_install_network_ui_wiring()`, called from `_ready()`
alongside the existing `_install_parental_gate_wiring()` call.

`main_scene.tscn`'s existing `UI` CanvasLayer (`[node name="UI" type="CanvasLayer" parent="."]`,
around line 201) already hosts multiple `instance=ExtResource(...)` children (`MobileOverlay`,
`Toast`, `Crosshair`, `Compass`, `HpBar`, `DeathScreen`, `InventorySlideIn`). `NetworkHud` (a
`VBoxContainer`, not its own `CanvasLayer`) belongs here, following the same
`[node name="NetworkHud" parent="UI" instance=ExtResource("N_network_hud")]` pattern.
`ConnectionProblemOverlay` and `JoinScreen` are each their own `CanvasLayer` (layer 110 / 20
respectively) — add them as direct children of the main_scene root (sibling of `UI`), not nested
inside the `UI` CanvasLayer, matching how a self-layered CanvasLayer should be parented.

`src/autoload/network_manager.gd:get_state() -> String` and `is_session_host() -> bool`
(existing, unchanged) are the exact checks needed to decide whether `main_scene.gd` should
instantiate `JoinScreen` on load (peer role, still connecting) versus skip it (host role, or
solo/no multiplayer).
