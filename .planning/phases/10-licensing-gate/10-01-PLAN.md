<objective>
Land a GPLv3 section 7 "App Store Distribution Exception" (additional permission) so an official signed Cubicraftia binary can be distributed through Apple's App Store, without changing GPL-3.0-or-later for every other distribution channel. This is licensing text and documentation only: no code, no signing, no submission work happens in this phase.

Purpose: Per `.planning/research/PITFALLS.md` HARD BLOCKER 1, Apple's App Store Terms of Service conflict with GPLv3 (the same conflict that got VLC and GNU Go pulled from the store). The fix, an explicit section 7 additional permission, is cheapest to add right now, while Cubicraftia's git history is 100% single-author (jnuyens). Landing it now, plus a CONTRIBUTING.md note, keeps the door open as external contributors arrive. This unblocks Phase 14 (SIGN-05) and Phase 16 (STORE-02); Phases 11-13 and the non-iOS parts of Phase 14 have no dependency on this phase and are unaffected either way.

Output: `LICENSES/LicenseRef-AppStore-Exception.txt`, `LICENSING.md`, an updated `README.md` License section, a new `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and an updated `.reuse/dep5`, all landed and REUSE-compliant relative to the pre-existing baseline. LICENSE-01 remains open pending attorney sign-off (tracked as this plan's final human-gate task).
</objective>
