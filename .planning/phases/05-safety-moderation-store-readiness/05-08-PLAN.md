## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-I1 | Information Disclosure | Reported user discovers they were reported | mitigate | No notification to reported user; auto-mute is local display filter only; RLS blocks reported_uid from seeing own reports row |
| T-05-D1 | Denial of Service | Mass-report spam via rapid button taps | mitigate | Server-side 5/24h rate limit in Go signaling (Plan 05-04); client-side submit button enters loading state after first tap |
