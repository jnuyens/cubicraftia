    # Cubicraftia — Privacy Policy

    ## Summary
    - We collect only what is needed to run the game and keep it safe.
    - We do not sell your data, show you ads, or share data with third parties.
    - You can delete your account and all data at any time.
    - Accounts for players under 13 require a parent's approval.

    ## 1. What We Collect
    **Account data:** email address, hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), username.

    **Social data:** friend list, blocked list, world ownership records.

    **Parental consent data (under-13 accounts only):** parent email address (used only to send the consent link), consent record with timestamp. The parent email is deleted when the account is deleted.

    **Crash and performance data (opt-in, anonymous):** device type, frame rate samples, crash reports. This data is not linked to your account.

    We do **not** collect: date of birth (only the derived "under 13" flag is stored), advertising identifiers, location data, or voice data.

    ## 2. How We Use Your Data
    Account data is used to authenticate you and run the friends and session features. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

    ## 3. Parental Consent (COPPA)
    If you declare that you are under 13, we ask for a parent or guardian email address to send a consent link. The email-plus method is used for parental consent, consistent with the FTC's COPPA guidelines. By clicking the consent link, the parent confirms: "I confirm that I am the parent or legal guardian of this account."

    ## 4. Data Retention
    We retain account data while your account is active. On account deletion, all data is deleted within 30 days. Reports submitted to the moderation queue are retained for 90 days.

    ## 5. Your Rights
    You may request a copy of your data by emailing us. You may delete your account from Settings at any time. The deletion is permanent after a 7-day grace period.

    ## 6. Discovery Server
    The Cubicraftia discovery server handles session matching and parental consent. It never stores game world content. The server source code is open source and available alongside the game.

    ## 7. Changes to This Policy
    We will notify you of changes by prompting you to review and accept the updated policy in-app.

    Keep under 2.5KB. CSAM reports paragraph: "Reports of child safety content are forwarded to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by US law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) and purged within 24 hours of forwarding." Include data-portability note: "A full data export is available on request via email for v1; an automated download-your-data feature is planned for a future update."</action>
